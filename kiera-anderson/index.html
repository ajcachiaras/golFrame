Made in small runs

Six people, one factory, four hundred pieces at a time.

We make everything with one family-run workshop outside Porto that has been sewing children's clothing since 1984. Small runs mean we can fix a pattern the week after we hear it is wrong — and that nothing ends up in a landfill because we guessed the demand badly.

  • Every style graded across nine sizes, fitted on real children rather than a mannequin
  • Flat seams, covered elastic and no scratchy labels, in every single piece
  • Offcuts go back into the beanies and the sock cuffs
