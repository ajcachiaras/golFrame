We started with one badly made pair of overalls.

Kiera Anderson is a small children's clothing label. This is the story we tell about why it exists — invented, like everything else on this demo site.

In 2019 our founder bought her nephew a pair of overalls that cost more than her own jeans. The strap button came off in a fortnight. The knee went in a month. She wrote to the company and never heard back, which turned out to be the useful part.

So we started making the version we wanted: cut roomier, reinforced where children actually wear through things, and priced so a second child can inherit it rather than a landfill. Four hundred pieces at a time, one workshop, nine sizes.

We are not a sustainable brand — we make clothes, which is not a neutral act. What we can do is make fewer of them, make them properly, and fix them when they break.

What we use

Four materials, chosen slowly

GOTS organic cotton

Grown without synthetic pesticides, spun in Portugal, dyed in a mill that recycles 92% of its water.

Extra-fine merino

Non-mulesed, 17.5 micron, blended with cotton so it stays soft against a toddler's neck.

PFAS-free waterproofing

Our rain shells are recycled polyester with a fluorocarbon-free finish. It needs reproofing every second winter, and we will tell you how.

Coconut & brass

Buttons from coconut shell, hardware in solid brass. Both outlive the garment, so both can be cut off and reused.

How we got here

Seven years, slowly

  1. 2019

    One pattern, one kitchen table

    Fifty pairs of overalls, sewn locally, sold to friends and the school gate.

  2. 2021

    The Porto workshop

    We found the six-person family factory we still work with, and moved everything to organic cotton.

  3. 2023

    Free repairs

    We started mending anything sent back to us, free, no receipt needed. 2,100 garments so far.

  4. 2024

    Nine sizes

    Extended the grade from 0–3 months to 10 years, fitted on 40 children rather than a mannequin.

  5. 2026

    The Orchard Collection

    Twelve pieces designed to layer with each other and nothing else. The one you are looking at.

Care & repairs

Send it back with a hole in it.

We will patch, re-seam, replace a zip or re-sew a strap button, free, for as long as we are still making the style. No receipt, no questions, and it does not have to have been bought new.

  • Wash cold, dry low — most wear comes from the dryer, not the child
  • Skip fabric softener; it coats the fibre and kills the stretch
  • Reproof rain shells every second winter with any PFAS-free spray

Shipping & returns: free US shipping over $75, flat $6 below that. Returns free for 60 days, worn or unworn.
